Account Deletion Request

If you want to delete your StepUp Carbon ID account and associated data, use one of the methods below.

Method 1: In-App Deletion (recommended)

Go to profile/settings and use the Delete Account option.

Method 2: Email Request

Send an email to REPLACE_WITH_SUPPORT_EMAIL with:

  • Subject: Delete My Account
  • Your registered email address
  • Optional reason (to help us improve)

Deletion Timeline

  • Account access removal: within 7 days
  • Primary data deletion: within 30 days
  • Some logs/backups may be retained temporarily for legal/security reasons

What Data Is Deleted

  • Authentication account data
  • User profile and app-related user records

What May Be Retained

  • Security or fraud-prevention logs (limited duration)
  • Records required by law
